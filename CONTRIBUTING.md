The agreement supports proprietary commercial and exclusive licensing, transfer to a company and sale of the underlying Map Services. Rights not effectively transferred remain with their holder, subject to the independent fallback licence where lawful; the agreement grants no licence back to assigned copyrights.

## Contribution process

1. Review [LICENSE](LICENSE), this document and [CLA.md](CLA.md).
2. Prepare a proposed contribution using the limited permission above. Identify all contributors and rights holders, any third-party materials, their sources, licences, notices and restrictions, and any prior rights grants affecting exclusivity. Obtain required employer and other rights-holder permissions.
3. Submit a pull request describing the change, relevant testing performed or omitted, and third-party disclosures (or state "None").
4. Explicitly accept the applicable CLA and arrange for all relevant assigning rights holders or authorized representatives and the Project Owner to execute the required written assignment covering the contribution and revisions.
5. Await review and possible acceptance. Before merge, the maintainer must verify acceptance, execution and complete coverage as described below. Maintainers may request revisions or decline a contribution.

## Explicit agreement and assignment execution

**External copyrightable material must not be merged until explicit CLA acceptance and an appropriately executed written copyright assignment cover the material from all relevant contributors or rights holders.** All relevant rights holders must execute through persons authorized to transfer their rights. An authorized employer or other rights-holder representative may execute for material it owns; the PR submitter cannot bind others without authority.

Acceptance may be recorded in the PR discussion using an identifiable account or in an identifiable written agreement privately retained by the Project Owner. Use this statement, replacing the placeholders:

> I agree to the MiniMap Contributor Assignment and License Agreement version 1.0 (CLA.md) for my contributions in [PR URL or precise contribution identifier] through [commit ID or identified revisions]. I agree to assign the copyrights and grant the fallback and patent licences specified in that agreement. I confirm my authority to do so and that all required employer and third-party permissions have been obtained. [If acting for a rights holder: its identity and my authority are recorded in the private agreement.]

**This statement records agreement; it is not a substitute for valid execution of the assignment.** Follow CLA section 3 to complete a written instrument intended as a deed of transfer, incorporating or attaching the exact agreed CLA text. Where Dutch law applies, satisfy Article 2 of the Auteurswet and the applicable deed requirements. Both the assigning party and the Project Owner must sign under the agreed process, using wet-ink or electronic execution that satisfies applicable legal requirements. A PR comment, checkbox, Git sign-off or submission alone must not be assumed to satisfy assignment formalities. Resolve uncertainty about execution before merge.

The privately retained assignment must identify the actual legal parties, the contribution, the agreement title and version, the exact agreed text, the revision or revisions covered, execution dates, signatures and relevant authority evidence. Link legal identities to contributor accounts and the Project Owner's public identity. The Project Owner must supply their actual legal identity and any necessary address or other execution details privately; no legal name or address is supplied by the public username.

Do not publish private personal information in PR discussions. Reference a privately retained signed agreement by a record identifier instead. The same executed instrument may record explicit acceptance and the assignment if it satisfies both requirements.

## Maintainer verification and records

Before merging, the maintainer must verify:

- Acceptance of version 1.0 of the Contributor Assignment and License Agreement and an appropriately executed written assignment for every relevant contributor or assigning rights holder, including co-authors and authors of included commits or patches.
- Coverage of all copyrightable contributed material at the proposed revisions. Additional contributed material or contributors require corresponding agreement and assignment coverage before merging.
- Legal authority and required employer or co-owner permissions, together with third-party disclosures, prior grants and restrictions that could affect ownership, exclusivity or commercial licensing. Third-party materials require separate review; one contributor's agreement cannot transfer or license another's rights.
- Retention by the Project Owner of the executed assignments, exact agreed CLA text, contribution and revision identifiers, and execution and authority evidence. Provide executing parties copies. Record the non-sensitive agreement reference, scope and verification in the PR.

The independent fallback licence protects against an ineffective, incomplete or unenforceable assignment, including unsatisfied formalities. It does not authorize accepting contributions without the required executed assignment, does not establish exclusive ownership where assignment fails, and does not grant contributors a licence back.

A template checkbox or PR comment can track completion but is not sufficient proof of a valid assignment. The Project Owner must retain and review the underlying execution evidence. Keep the exact agreed version with each record; later agreement changes do not retroactively alter earlier terms.

Ordinary bug reports, feature suggestions and technical discussions do not require agreement or assignment unless copyrightable material from them is incorporated into MiniMap. Obtain the required coverage before merging such material.
